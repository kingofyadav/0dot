1. WHAT IS 0DOT.IN
================================================================================

0dot.in is an all-in-one identity and creator platform. Every account gets
one permanent, public URL:

    0dot.in/yourname

That single link is simultaneously your social profile, your link-in-bio
page, your portfolio, your storefront, and the front door to anything else
you build on the platform — a community, a business, or an organization.
The username is permanent: once claimed, it is never reassigned while your
account is active (see §2.3).

Everything in this guide hangs off that one identity.

================================================================================
2. GETTING STARTED
================================================================================

2.1  CREATING AN ACCOUNT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Go to 0dot.in/signup. You'll provide:

  - Full name
  - Username           (0dot.in/yourname — permanent, 3-30 chars, letters/
                         numbers/underscore only)
  - Email
  - Password           (8+ characters)
  - Mobile number       (with country code)
  - Date of birth

The username field checks availability live as you type. Reserved and
platform-brand words can never be claimed.

Signup is rate-limited per IP and per email, and a duplicate signup with an
existing email, username, or phone number is rejected with a clear message
telling you which field collided.

2.2  VERIFYING YOUR EMAIL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
After signup you land on a "check your email" screen and a verification
link is sent. You are logged in immediately (you can browse), but full
posting/interaction is expected only after verification. If the email
doesn't arrive, the same screen has a resend option.

2.3  CLAIMING A USERNAME
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Normally, username claiming happens as part of signup (above) and can never
be separated from your account. There is, however, a dedicated recovery
route for the rare case where an account exists without one:

    0dot.in/claim-username

If you ever land in a state where your account has no working profile page
(your own username 404s, or Settings is unreachable), this is the page that
fixes it — enter your full name and desired username and submit. This is
the correct, supported way to attach a username to an account; it should
never require manual intervention.

Renaming an existing username is NOT supported — usernames are permanent by
design (this is a deliberate product decision, not a missing feature).

2.4  LOGGING IN, 2FA, AND PASSWORD RESET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Log in:            0dot.in/login
                      Accepts email, username, OR mobile number as the
                      identifier, plus your password.

  Two-factor auth:    Settings > Security > Two-factor authentication
                      Sets up an authenticator-app (TOTP) code plus a set
                      of one-time recovery codes shown once at enrollment.
                      Once enabled, login requires the code at
                      0dot.in/login/2fa after your password succeeds.

  Forgot password:    0dot.in/forgot-password
                      Enter your email; a reset link is emailed to you
                      (valid 1 hour, single-use). Follow it to
                      0dot.in/reset-password?token=... and set a new
                      password there.

  IMPORTANT: if you're logging in with a reset password and keep getting
  "Incorrect email/username/mobile number or password," double-check you're
  typing your EMAIL (or the username you actually claimed) as the
  identifier — a mistyped or unclaimed username will always fail this
  check even with the correct password, and looks identical to a wrong
  password from the error message alone.

  Active sessions:    Settings > Security > Active sessions
                      Lists every device with a live session; revoke any
                      one remotely except the device you're on now.

  Login history:      Same page — shows your recent login attempts,
                      including failed ones, so you can spot anything you
                      didn't do.

2.5  YOUR ACCOUNT SETTINGS MAP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Everything account-related lives under:

    0dot.in/s/yourname

organized into these groups (this is the exact structure of the settings
sidebar):

  Profile        Edit profile, Links
  Security       Change password, Two-factor authentication, Active
                 sessions, Email & phone
  Portfolio      Projects, Skills, Resume, Repositories, Credentials, Layout
  Monetization   Payouts, Memberships, Digital products, Freelance
                 services, Affiliate
  Billing        Premium, Custom domain
  Content        Articles, Wiki & Docs, Books, Files, Courses, Learning
                 paths, Podcast, Newsletter, Livestreams
  Tools          Business card, Short links, Calendar, Forms & surveys,
                 Auto-post
  Developer      Apps, Authorized apps
  Notifications  Push & delivery
  Privacy        Privacy, Blocked users
  Account        Account management (export / deactivate / delete),
                 Language & accessibility

This settings shell only becomes reachable once your account has a claimed
username (§2.3) — it's addressed at 0dot.in/s/<your-username>.

3. YOUR PROFILE
================================================================================

3.1  PROFILE BASICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Settings > Profile > Edit profile lets you set:

  - Display name, bio
  - Avatar and cover photo
  - Theme preset (a curated set of accent color / background / font
    combinations — not free-form CSS; this is a deliberate security
    choice, not a limitation you can lift)
  - Social links row (X/Twitter, Instagram, GitHub, etc. from a fixed
    platform allowlist)

A Premium subscription (§6.10) unlocks a larger library of curated theme
presets — still token-based, never raw markup.

3.2  LINKS (LINK-IN-BIO)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Settings > Profile > Links. Add, edit, delete, and drag-to-reorder as many
links as you like (up to a soft cap; Premium raises it). Each link can be:

  - Featured (a small number can be visually pinned above the rest)
  - Scheduled (set a start/end window — it's invisible to visitors outside
    that window but stays visible, grayed out, to you)

Every public click is recorded (no IP/user-agent stored) and rolled up
into click analytics you can see as the owner. Free tier shows the
trailing 30 days; Premium shows full history.

3.3  CUSTOM DOMAIN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Settings > Billing > Custom domain (requires an active Premium or Business
subscription — one domain is included). Point your own domain
(yourname.com) at 0dot's edge via the DNS instructions shown; once it
verifies, your entire profile (articles, projects, everything) mirrors at
your domain in addition to 0dot.in/yourname, which always keeps working.
Note: your custom domain is read-only browsing — likes, comments, and
follows always happen back on 0dot.in, where your session actually lives.

4. POSTING, FEED, AND DISCOVERY
================================================================================

4.1  POSTS, REPLIES, REPOSTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Compose from the Feed sidebar or the "+" compose action. A post is up to
500 characters of text and/or up to 4 images/video. You can:

  - Like / unlike
  - Bookmark (private — 0dot.in/bookmarks, never visible to anyone else)
  - Reply (one level of threading)
  - Repost, with or without added commentary (quote-repost)

Hashtags (#like-this) and @mentions are parsed automatically from what you
type.

4.2  FEED, EXPLORE, TRENDING, SEARCH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  0dot.in/feed       Posts from people you follow, plus your own — your
                     personalized home timeline.
  0dot.in/explore    Global, chronological feed across all public posts —
                     good for finding accounts you don't follow yet.
  0dot.in/trending   Ranked by engagement velocity (what's accelerating
                     right now), not just what's newest or most-liked
                     overall.
  0dot.in/search     Spans users, posts, communities, businesses, projects,
                     articles/docs, events, and marketplace listings.

4.3  FOLLOW, NOTIFICATIONS, MESSAGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Follow             One click on any profile — no approval step needed
                     (profiles are public by default).
  Notifications      0dot.in/notifications — likes, comments, mentions,
                     new followers, messages, and more, aggregated so
                     "Alice and 12 others liked your post" doesn't flood
                     you with one row per like.
  Messages           0dot.in/messages — direct and group chats. A message
                     from someone you don't follow (and who doesn't
                     follow you) lands in a separate Requests list, not
                     your main inbox, until you accept it. Content is
                     encrypted at rest.

4.4  BLOCKING AND REPORTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Block from any profile's menu — this removes any existing follow between
you both, stops them starting new conversations with you, and suppresses
their notifications to you. Report any post, profile, or other content via
the report action available wherever content appears; your identity as the
reporter is never disclosed to the person you reported.

4.5  JOBS BOARD & MAP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Two platform-wide surfaces that aggregate what already exists elsewhere —
no separate data to maintain.

  0dot.in/jobs       A cross-business job board. Every opening any active
                     Business has posted (§8) shows up here in one list,
                     filterable by keyword, location, remote-only, and
                     employment type (full-time / part-time / contract /
                     internship). Applying still happens on the business's
                     own Jobs tab.
  0dot.in/jobs/alerts  Save a search (keywords / location / remote /
                     employment type) as a standing alert and get notified
                     when a new matching opening is posted. Manage or
                     delete your alerts from the same page. (Login
                     required.)
  0dot.in/map        A map view of every geo-tagged Business location and
                     every upcoming geo-tagged Event, rendered as pins you
                     can click through to the business or event page.

5. CONTENT & CREATOR TOOLS
================================================================================
All published under your own profile, e.g. 0dot.in/yourname/articles.

5.1  ARTICLES, TUTORIALS, NOTES  —  Settings > Content > Articles
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
One unified long-form writing tool covering three formats you pick per
piece:
  - Article   — a polished, typically public long-form post
  - Tutorial  — a single how-to document (not a multi-lesson course — for
                that, see Courses in §6.7)
  - Note      — a short, casual, typically PRIVATE personal note

Each piece has a real three-tier visibility: Public, Unlisted (viewable
only by direct link, not listed/searchable), or Private (genuinely
access-controlled — never returned to anyone but you, by any path,
including search).

5.2  WIKI & DOCUMENTATION  —  Settings > Content > Wiki & Docs
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
A personal, nested, version-history-tracked page system (docs get a
persistent table-of-contents sidebar; wiki presents more free-form). Every
edit keeps its full revision history. You are the sole editor of your own
personal wiki — for multi-author collaborative editing, create a private
Community (§7) instead, which has its own multi-editor wiki.

5.3  BOOKS  —  Settings > Content > Books
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Author chapters natively (reusing the same wiki/revision engine as §5.2)
and/or upload a finished ebook file (epub/pdf) — either path is valid on
its own, or combine both.

5.4  FILES  —  Settings > Content > Files
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Publish a finished document as-is (report, whitepaper, zine). Public files
get a stable, permanent URL; unlisted/private files are served through a
short-lived, authorization-checked download link.

5.5  PODCAST  —  Settings > Content > Podcast
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Publish episodes with a real public RSS feed (works in Apple Podcasts,
Spotify, etc.). Member-only bonus episodes are supported via a private
per-subscriber feed URL — treat that private link as a secret; anyone who
gets it can access gated episodes until you revoke it.

5.6  NEWSLETTER  —  Settings > Content > Newsletter
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Compose and send issues to subscribers (0dot account holders or email-only
subscribers). Every send includes a one-click unsubscribe, honored
immediately. Issues can be gated to a membership tier.

5.7  PORTFOLIO  —  Settings > Portfolio
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Your profile can double as a professional portfolio:

  Projects       0dot.in/p/your-project-slug — title, description,
                 gallery, collaborators (who don't need a 0dot account to
                 be credited), status, and visibility (public/unlisted).
  Skills         A free-text, endorsable skill list — anyone logged in
                 can endorse a skill once.
  Resume         Renders from your Work Experience + Education + Skills +
                 any project you've marked "featured on resume" — no
                 second, disconnected data entry. You can also attach an
                 uploaded PDF resume alongside it.
  Repositories   Link public GitHub/GitLab/Bitbucket repos; stars/
                 description/language sync periodically from the
                 provider. Public repos only — no OAuth/private-repo
                 linking.
  Credentials    Certificates, awards, and research papers — self-
                 attested with an optional external verification link
                 (like LinkedIn); 0dot does not itself verify authenticity
                 with the issuing body.
  Layout         Reorder or hide any portfolio section on your public
                 profile.

6. MONETIZATION
================================================================================

6.1  GETTING PAID: PAYOUT ACCOUNTS  —  Settings > Monetization > Payouts
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every money-earning feature below (memberships, tips, digital products,
freelance bookings, affiliate commissions, course sales, event tickets you
sell as a host) shares ONE payout account and one payment ledger. Set this
up once here — identity verification (KYC) and tax reporting are handled
entirely by the payment processor's own onboarding, not by 0dot directly.
You cannot receive a payout-requiring transaction until this account is
active.

6.2  MEMBERSHIPS & PAID SUBSCRIPTIONS  —  Monetization > Memberships
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Create tiers (name, price, monthly or yearly billing) that fans subscribe
to. A single "required tier" gate is reused everywhere gated content
exists — gated posts, bonus podcast episodes, course access, paid
livestreams — so one subscription can unlock all of it at once at the
right tier level. Cancelling retains access through the end of the current
billing period, never immediately.

6.3  TIPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
A one-time payment with an optional public thank-you message, available
from any creator's profile once they have an active payout account. The
simplest monetization feature — no subscription state, just a single
charge.

6.4  DIGITAL PRODUCTS  —  Monetization > Digital products
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sell downloadable files (an ebook, a preset pack, a template) for a fixed
price. Buyers get a short-lived, authorization-checked download link —
never a permanently public URL — issued fresh only to verified buyers.

6.5  FREELANCE SERVICES & APPOINTMENTS  —  Monetization > Freelance services
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
List a bookable service with a duration; set your weekly availability.
Customers request a time slot; you confirm it (nothing auto-confirms into
your calendar). Two confirmed bookings can never overlap. This is
single-session, paid-up-front booking — there's no multi-milestone
project/escrow system.

6.6  AFFILIATE PROGRAM  —  Monetization > Affiliate
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Run a commission program on your own membership tiers, digital products,
or courses: set a commission %, and any 0dot user with their own payout
account can generate a referral link and earn a cut on sales they drive
(last-click attribution, 30-day window).

6.7  COURSES & LEARNING PATHS  —  Content > Courses / Learning paths
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Build a structured course (modules > lessons: video, text, or downloadable
file). Sell it directly (one-time price) and/or bundle it into a
membership tier — either grants access, and losing a membership only
revokes membership-granted access, never a direct purchase. Group several
courses into a Learning Path with quizzes; completing one auto-issues a
Credential (see §5.7) to the learner's portfolio.

6.8  LIVESTREAMS  —  Content > Livestreams
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Single-host live broadcast with live chat, optionally gated to a
membership tier, with optional recording (if you enable recording, every
participant sees a clear, unmissable notice before/at the start — this is
a legal requirement, not optional UI).

6.9  COIN WALLET, UPI TOP-UP & COIN TRANSFERS  —  0dot.in/wallet
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
An alternate, cash-first way to fund a Premium subscription (§6.10) for
users who'd rather not use a card:

  1. Pick a coin amount (1 coin = $1, fixed peg) — you get a QR code /
     UPI deep link to pay the platform directly.
  2. Pay via any UPI app, then type in the UTR (transaction reference)
     your UPI app gave you as proof.
  3. An admin manually verifies it against the platform's bank statement
     and approves — your coin balance is credited.
  4. Spend coins on Premium at Settings > Billing > Premium — a
     coin-funded Premium subscription grants identical perks to a
     card-funded one.

You can also send coins directly to another account from the wallet
("Send coins" — enter their username and an amount, rate-limited and
capped per transfer), and request a payout of your coin balance back to a
UPI VPA (minimum 50 coins) — also manually reviewed and paid by an admin.
New accounts start with a small welcome bonus.

6.10  PREMIUM PROFILE  —  Settings > Billing > Premium
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
A personal paid tier (monthly or yearly, payable by card via Stripe or by
coins, §6.9). Perks:
  - One custom domain included (§3.3)
  - Full link-click analytics history (free tier: 30 days)
  - Higher link cap, and a larger curated theme-preset library
  - A distinct Premium badge (never a substitute for account verification
    — those are two different, never-conflated signals)
  - A reduced platform fee on your own creator earnings

Cancelling is non-destructive: links beyond the free cap go inactive
rather than deleted, and reactivate instantly if you resubscribe;
analytics data itself is never deleted, only the visible window narrows.

6.11  FUNDRAISERS & DONATIONS  —  0dot.in/fund/new
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Run a donation campaign — a cause, a project, a personal ask. Start one at
0dot.in/fund/new with a title, short description, optional cover image, an
optional goal amount (leave it blank for an open-ended, ongoing
fundraiser), and an optional end date. It lives at 0dot.in/fund/<id> with
a running total and progress bar.

  - Donations flow through the SAME payout account and payment ledger as
    everything else in §6 — set up Payouts (§6.1) before you can receive
    any. A single donation is between $1 and $5,000.
  - Donors can leave an optional public thank-you message, and can choose
    to donate anonymously — an anonymous donation hides the donor's name
    from the public campaign page, but the organizer still sees who gave.
  - The organizer can cancel a campaign at any time, which stops it
    accepting further donations.
  - Fundraisers have no central directory — share the campaign link
    directly. In this release a fundraiser is hosted by an individual
    account (business- and organization-hosted fundraisers are planned).

7. COMMUNITIES  (0dot.in/c)
================================================================================

Communities are shared spaces multiple people organize around — distinct
from your personal profile.

BROWSE:          0dot.in/c — your communities plus a discovery list of
                  public ones (full filtering lives in Search, §4.2).

CREATE ONE:      0dot.in/c/new
                  Set a name, unique slug (0dot.in/c/your-slug),
                  description, and visibility:
                    Public      — anyone can view and join instantly
                    Restricted  — anyone can view; joining needs
                                  moderator approval
                    Private     — content visible to members only (the
                                  community's existence is still
                                  searchable by name)

ROLES:            Owner (you, at creation — full control, including
                  deleting the community), Moderator (remove posts/
                  comments, mute/ban members, edit rules & wiki, pin
                  posts), Member.

WHAT YOU CAN BUILD INSIDE A COMMUNITY:
  - A shared feed (the same post/like/comment/repost mechanics as your
    personal feed, scoped to the community)
  - Rules (an ordered list, editable by moderators)
  - Discovery tags + per-post flair (moderator-defined labels like
    "Question"/"Announcement")
  - Polls (single or multi-choice, closeable)
  - Q&A threads (mark a reply as the accepted answer)
  - A community wiki (multi-editor, full revision history —
    moderators-only or any-member editing, your choice)
  - Live chat (a real-time broadcast channel, distinct from DMs)
  - Voice rooms (scheduled live audio sessions, speaker/listener roles)
  - Lightweight bulletin-board events, or a full ticketed Event hosted
    by the community (§10)

MODERATION:       Every moderator action (remove post, mute/ban member,
                  edit rule/wiki, pin) is logged to an auditable modlog.
                  There's no member-facing appeals flow inside a
                  community — for platform-policy-level disputes, escalate
                  through the general report/appeal system (§15).

MANAGE:            0dot.in/c/your-slug — moderator/owner controls surface
                  directly on the community page for anyone with the
                  right role.

8. BUSINESSES  (0dot.in/b)
================================================================================

A Business page represents a company, not a person — with its own team,
catalog, and public presence.

BROWSE:           0dot.in/b — your businesses (including ones still under
                  review) plus a discovery list of active ones.

CREATE ONE:       0dot.in/b/new
                  Because a business page can be impersonated to damage a
                  real company's reputation, creation goes through a
                  claim/review gate before it's publicly live — this is
                  by design, not a bug if your new business page doesn't
                  appear instantly searchable.

TEAM & ROLES:     Settings on the business page. Owner (full control,
                  including transfer/delete), Admin (manage team, catalog,
                  jobs, appointments, respond to reviews, post as the
                  business), Editor (manage catalog/posts/jobs, no team/
                  billing control), Member (internal-only association,
                  optionally listed publicly on the Team tab — listing and
                  write-permission are independent settings).

POSTING AS THE BUSINESS: Anyone Editor+ can publish a post that displays
                  with the business's name/logo instead of their own —
                  your individual account stays recorded internally for
                  accountability, just not shown publicly.

WHAT A BUSINESS PAGE INCLUDES:
  - Company info: locations & hours, contact info, category
  - Products & Services (a unified catalog — services can be made
    bookable)
  - Store: a storefront view of your catalog with in-app checkout
    (0dot.in/b/your-slug/store)
  - Jobs: post openings, review applications (0dot.in/b/your-slug/jobs);
    open postings also appear on the platform-wide job board (§4.5)
  - Appointments: bookable services with staff availability and
    confirm/decline
  - Reviews: public star ratings + text, one official response per
    review allowed — you cannot delete or hide a negative review
  - Documents: a file library (brochures, spec sheets), public or
    team-only
  - A business post feed and business-authored content

CONTACT FORM:      Visitors can message the business without an account
                  (name + email required if logged out); Admin+ team
                  members see submissions as a queue.

CUSTOM DOMAIN:     Same mechanism as a personal profile (§3.3), gated on
                  an active business subscription, managed by Owner/Admin.

9. ORGANIZATIONS  (0dot.in/org)
================================================================================

An Organization is different from a Business: it's a company's INTERNAL
control plane over its own employees' accounts — not a public storefront.
You can have a Business, an Organization, both linked together, or just
one — they're independent.

BROWSE:            0dot.in/org — the organizations you belong to.
CREATE ONE:        0dot.in/org/new

WHAT AN ORGANIZATION GIVES YOU:
  - Team/employee management, independent of any Business team roles
  - Internal communities: an existing Community can be restricted so only
    active organization members can join/view it
  - Single sign-on (SSO): connect your company's own identity provider
    (SAML2/OIDC — Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace, etc.) so employees log
    in with corporate credentials. IMPORTANT: enabling SSO enforcement
    never removes an employee's own non-employer login method for their
    personal account — losing your job never locks you out of your own
    0dot identity, only organization-scoped resources.
  - An internal employee directory (name, title, department, contact) —
    visible only to fellow active members of the same organization, never
    public
  - An audit log of organization-level actions (member changes, SSO
    config changes, community changes) — visible only to that
    organization's own admins

MANAGE:            0dot.in/org/your-org-id/manage
AUDIT LOG:         0dot.in/org/your-org-id/audit-log

Deactivating an employee promptly removes their access to org-restricted
communities and SSO sessions — it never touches their personal profile,
posts, or any content built outside the organization's context.

10. EVENTS & TICKETING  (0dot.in/e)
================================================================================

BROWSE:            0dot.in/e — upcoming events, soonest first.
CREATE ONE:        0dot.in/e/new
                  Hosted by you personally, by a Business, or by a
                  Community — pick one host. Format: in-person, virtual,
                  or hybrid. For a virtual/hybrid event you can simply
                  link out to an external meeting tool (Zoom, Meet) — no
                  native streaming required to launch an event.

RSVP:              Free "going / interested / not going." If the event
                  has a capacity, "going" is capped once full (no
                  waitlist) — "interested" always stays open.

TICKETING:         Paid ticket tiers (General, VIP, etc.), using the same
                  payment ledger as everything else in §6. Each ticket
                  gets a genuine, unguessable QR check-in code. NOTE:
                  community-hosted events are RSVP-only — paid ticketing
                  requires a personal or business host.

NATIVE LIVE SESSION: Optionally attach a livestream or voice room to the
                  event, with the same recording-consent disclosure
                  requirement as §6.8.

Attendee list visibility (public / attendees-only / host-only) is
configurable per event.

================================================================================
11. MARKETPLACE  (0dot.in/m)
================================================================================

Browse: 0dot.in/m — a single cross-category storefront spanning:
  - Courses and Digital products (surfaced from what creators already
    sell on their own profiles — buying here is identical to buying
    directly from them)
  - Freelance services (bookable individual services, §6.5)
  - Themes, Templates, and Apps — third-party listings

SELL A LISTING:    0dot.in/m/new (Themes/Templates/Apps category)
                  Every listing goes through a review gate before going
                  live — there is no direct-to-live publish path.
                    - Themes: still the same token-based preset schema
                      as your own profile theme — never raw CSS/HTML.
                    - Templates: applying one pre-fills a normal creation
                      form and goes through the same validation as typing
                      it yourself.
                    - Apps: sandboxed, declarative widgets by default
                      (safe embeds, no arbitrary code); a listing can
                      optionally be backed by a real OAuth-integrated
                      Developer App (§14) for deeper functionality.

INSTALL AN APP:    0dot.in/m/[id]/install — installs onto your profile,
                  a business page, or a community.

REVIEWS:           You can only review a listing you've actually
                  purchased or installed (verified-purchase gating) —
                  this is stricter than Business reviews (§8), which
                  anyone can leave.

13. AI TOOLS
================================================================================

AI features appear inline wherever they're useful (e.g. "Write my bio with
AI" and "Draft & translate articles" in the Feed sidebar) rather than as a
separate app:

  AI profile/content writer   Produces a draft (bio, article, post,
                              business description) that lands in the
                              normal compose box for you to review and
                              edit — AI output is never saved or published
                              directly; it goes through the exact same
                              validation a manually-typed draft would.
  AI translation              On-demand translation of your published
                              content into another language, cached per
                              version — editing the source invalidates any
                              stale cached translation automatically.
  AI accessibility            Automatic alt-text/captions generated for
                              new media uploads (never blocking your
                              upload) — an AI-written caption you manually
                              correct is never silently overwritten by a
                              later re-generation.
  AI moderation                Runs behind the scenes to flag potentially
                              policy-violating content for human review —
                              it never auto-removes ordinary content by
                              itself; a person always makes the final call.
  AI search & recommendations   A supplementary ranking signal blended
                              into ordinary search/suggested-users results
                              — private/unlisted content is never exposed
                              through this layer any more than it already
                              is through regular search.

Every AI feature invocation is logged for audit purposes; content you mark
private stays private through the AI pipeline as well — nothing you keep
private becomes queryable or exposed through an AI feature.

14. DEVELOPER PLATFORM
================================================================================

For anyone building on top of 0dot rather than just using it.

REGISTER AN APP:   Settings > Developer > Apps
                  Get a client ID + a one-time-shown client secret, and
                  register your redirect URIs (exact-match only).

"SIGN IN WITH 0DOT":  Standard OAuth2 authorization-code + PKCE flow.
                  Users can see and revoke any app's access at any time
                  from Settings > Developer > Authorized apps — this
                  includes 0dot's own first-party mobile/desktop apps,
                  which authenticate through the identical mechanism as
                  any third-party integration (no privileged shortcut).

PUBLIC API:        0dot.in/api/v1/... — a versioned REST API covering
                  posts, profiles, feed, messages, notifications, search,
                  wallet, marketplace, and more. It enforces the exact
                  same visibility/privacy rules as the website itself
                  (private content, blocks, gated content) — there is no
                  separate, looser access layer for API consumers.

WEBHOOKS:          Subscribe your app to platform events (the same event
                  catalog that drives in-app notifications) and receive
                  signed HTTP callbacks — scoped strictly to what a given
                  user has actually authorized your app to see.

Sensitive scopes (e.g. reading private messages, posting on a user's
behalf) require your app to pass a review step before you can even
request them from users.

15. TRUST & SAFETY, COPYRIGHT/DMCA
================================================================================

REPORT:            One consistent "Report" action is available on any
                  reportable content or account across the whole platform
                  (0dot.in/trust-safety and inline wherever content
                  appears). Your identity as the reporter is never
                  disclosed to the person you report.

APPEAL:            If enforcement action is taken against you, you can
                  file an appeal (0dot.in/admin/trust-safety/appeals is
                  the staff-facing queue; the appeal itself is filed from
                  wherever the enforcement notice appeared). Reviewed, by
                  policy, by someone other than the original decision-
                  maker wherever staffing allows.

COPYRIGHT / DMCA:  0dot.in/dmca — file a formal takedown notice if your
                  copyrighted work was posted without permission, or file
                  a counter-notice if your own content was taken down in
                  error. Unlike ordinary reports, a DMCA complainant's
                  contact information IS shared with the person who filed
                  a counter-notice — this is a legal requirement of the
                  process, not a privacy inconsistency.

AGE:               New accounts collect date of birth at signup; accounts
                  with no verified age are held to default protective
                  restrictions until it's provided.

16. MOBILE APPS
================================================================================

0dot ships native iOS and Android apps (built on Expo/React Native) plus
an installable PWA (the desktop "app" is this same installable web app,
not a separate codebase).

  - Every mobile screen mirrors a web feature 1:1 — feed, profile,
    messages, communities, businesses, marketplace, wallet, events,
    settings (two-factor, sessions, blocked users, privacy, account
    management) all have direct mobile equivalents.
  - The apps authenticate via the exact same OAuth2 + PKCE flow described
    in §14 — not a separate, privileged login path.
  - Universal/App Links: sharing any 0dot.in URL (a profile, a community,
    a business, a project, an event) opens directly in the installed app
    when you have it, and falls back to the mobile browser otherwise.
  - Push notifications cover the same event catalog as in-app
    notifications and email — manage delivery channel per notification
    type at Settings > Notifications. A push notification about private
    content (a DM, a private note) is always generic ("You have a new
    message from Alex") and never shows the private content itself on
    your lock screen.
  - Face ID / fingerprint unlock is available as a local convenience layer
    in front of your already-logged-in session — it's not a new login
    method for the account itself.

17. FOR PLATFORM OPERATORS  (admin / platform roles)
================================================================================

This section is for 0dot's own staff, not end users.

PLATFORM ROLES:     0dot.in/admin/platform-roles (super_admin only)
                  A single ranked role — support < admin < super_admin —
                  grants staff access to admin tooling in-app. This
                  replaced two separate legacy mechanisms (a platform-
                  admin boolean flag and a separate trust & safety staff
                  role) that were unified into one system.

TRUST & SAFETY QUEUE:  0dot.in/admin/trust-safety
                  A unified staff case queue covering content reports,
                  account reports, business-claim reviews, marketplace-
                  listing reviews, sensitive OAuth-scope requests, and
                  AI-flagged content — one workflow instead of several
                  separate ones.
  Appeals:          0dot.in/admin/trust-safety/appeals
  Transparency:      0dot.in/admin/trust-safety/transparency
                    (aggregate case-volume/resolution reporting, including
                    the appeal-overturn rate as an accountability signal)

WALLET REVIEW:      0dot.in/admin/wallet/topups and .../payouts
                  Manual UPI top-up/payout approval queue (§6.9) — an
                  admin's own verification against the bank statement is
                  the entire fraud control in this flow; there is no
                  automated reconciliation.

PAYMENTS:           0dot.in/admin/payments/iap-batches
                  Reconciliation for mobile in-app-purchase revenue
                  (Apple/Google take their own cut independent of and in
                  addition to the platform fee; this arrives as an
                  aggregated lump sum that has to be tracked back to
                  individual creators before payout).
